# FAMILY.md - the one-page brief for parents and guardians

**What IEP Ally is.** A free assistant that runs on your own computer and helps you build and keep your child's IEP plan: the about-me picture, the goals, the support team, your notes, and a one-page brief for meetings. It is the IEP Companion Generator app's thinking, rebuilt as a personal agent you talk to.

**What it needs from you.** A first conversation (the intake) where it asks about your child, your existing IEP or reports, and your term dates. You can paste text from reports straight into the chat. That is the setup. After that, you use it whenever something changes or a meeting is coming.

**Where your data lives.** In one folder on your computer. Not on a server, not in an account, not with us. You can open the files, back them up, move them, or delete them. If you run the agent on a local model, nothing leaves the machine at all.

**What it will never do.**
- It will not diagnose your child, or promise outcomes.
- It will not give legal or medical advice. It helps you get ready for the people who do.
- It will not contact anyone unless you tell it to, in the moment, by name.
- It will not make you feel behind. Two minutes a day is a normal way to use it.

**On the day of a meeting.** Ask the agent for a Meeting Prep. You will get a one-page brief: your points, your questions, the goals you are holding to, and what to watch for. Print it and take it in.

**Free, and private, by design.** The agent is free software (PolyForm Noncommercial, free for personal and noncommercial use). If it ever stops being useful, you stop using it, and your folder stays yours.
