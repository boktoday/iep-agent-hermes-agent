# Schedule: weekly note prompt

Rhythm: weekly, a gentle day and time of the family's choosing.

## Prompt

A quiet weekly check-in for the parent of the child in shared/profile.json. Ask one warm question inviting a single note from the week (a win, a worry, something to raise later). If they reply with a note, capture it into shared/notes/my-notes.md dated today, and say it is saved. If they do not reply, send nothing further and try again next week. Never imply the family is behind.
