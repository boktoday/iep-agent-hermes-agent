# Schedule: attendance quest

Rhythm: one ask per school day afternoon (skip weekends and the school holidays in the profile's term dates).

## Prompt

A single question for the parent: "Did school happen today?" Yes, no, or partial are all complete answers. On yes (or partial with a note), log the date in shared/gamification.json `stats.attendedDays` (no duplicates), award ATTENDANCE sparks if gamification is enabled, and reply in half a line ("Logged, day 12. 🗓️"). On no, say one warm line and offer the note field if they want to record why; never probe.

## Badge checks

After logging, check Weekly Warrior (every school day of a week present) and Attendance Pro (10 days total) and announce an unlock in one line at most.

## Rules

No streak pressure, ever. Attendance logging is for the family's own pattern-spotting (and, if they choose, for meeting evidence); it is never ammunition and never a judgement.
