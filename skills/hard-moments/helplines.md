# Helplines and real people

Use the parent's country from `shared/profile.json`. If the country is unknown, ask once, kindly. These are verified public services, current as at October 2026. They are not the agent's help; they are real people.

## Australia

- **Emergency:** 000
- **Lifeline:** 13 11 14 (24/7) or lifeline.org.au
- **Beyond Blue:** 1300 22 4636 (24/7) or beyondblue.org.au
- **Kids Helpline** (for the young person): 1800 55 1800 (24/7) or kidshelpline.com.au
- **Parentline** (state-based parenting support): 1300 30 1300 (QLD) or parentline.com.au for your state's line
- **1800RESPECT** (family violence and sexual assault): 1800 737 732 (24/7)
- **MensLine Australia:** 1300 78 99 78
- **Carer Gateway** (carer support and counselling): 1800 422 737 or carergateway.gov.au

## New Zealand

- **Emergency:** 111
- **Need to Talk?** 1737 (call or text, 24/7)
- **Lifeline Aotearoa:** 0800 543 354

## United Kingdom

- **Emergency:** 999
- **Samaritans:** 116 123 (24/7)
- **NSPCC** (concerns about a child): 0808 800 5000

## United States and Canada

- **Emergency:** 911 (US) / 911 (Canada)
- **988 Suicide and Crisis Lifeline:** call or text 988 (US, 24/7)
- **Canada Suicide Prevention Service:** 988

## Everywhere

- **findahelpline.com** lists verified helplines by country.
- The child's own support team: GP, paediatrician, school counsellor, and the trusted adults already named in the plan.

If someone is in immediate danger, the first line is always the local emergency number. Say that plainly and early.
