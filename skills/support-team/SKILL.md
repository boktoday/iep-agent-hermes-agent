# Support Team

Keep `supportTeam[]` in `shared/profile.json` accurate: `{ name, role, contact }` per person.

## Who usually belongs

Parents and guardians first, then the teacher, aide or education assistant, the coordinator or head of inclusion, therapists (speech, OT, psychology), and anyone the family trusts at meetings. Keep former team members only if the family wants the history.

## Updating

1. Take additions as free text ("Mrs Chan is the new aide, she started week 3").
2. Split into name, role, contact; ask only for what is missing ("best contact for her: school line or email?").
3. Read back and save.

## At meeting time

Meeting Prep uses this list to spell names and roles correctly. Before a meeting, ask once: "Is the support team list current? Anyone new since last time?"

## Rules

- Contact details stay in the local file only; the agent never messages anyone from this list unless the parent asks it to, in the moment, by name.
- No judgements about people in this file. Feelings about the school belong in My Notes, phrased carefully, not in the team list.
