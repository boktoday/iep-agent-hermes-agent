# About Me

Keep the child's About Me picture current in `shared/profile.json`. These fields carry the whole plan, so they get written with care.

## The fields

- `diagnosis[]` - considerations as the parent frames them. Label it honestly in conversation ("the reports name X; we can write it as 'X (as described in the 2025 assessment)' or leave it out; your call").
- `strengths[]` - at least three. The child's own words if anyone has them. These open every document.
- `challenges[]` - specific and kind. "Multi-step instructions spoken once, in a noisy room" beats "poor listener".
- `whatHelpsLearn[]` - the practical wins: visual schedules, movement breaks, instructions in writing, chunked tasks, quiet seats.
- `howToCommunicate[]` - how the child communicates best, how to check understanding, what to do when words stop working.
- `keyStaffMember` - the person at school who knows the child best.

## Adding or changing an entry

1. Ask one focused question (or take the free-text dump and sort it).
2. Transform the answer into a clean line, and read it back: "Saving under What Helps: 'written instructions with a picture schedule'. Right?"
3. Write it, and say what changed in one line.

## Writing rules

- Strengths appear in every export. Never let a document go out strengths-lite.
- Challenges are described through the environment as often as the child ("the classroom is loud and instructions come fast" is easier to act on than "she can't focus").
- No medical labels beyond what the parent uses; no speculation about causes.
- The dignity test: if the child read this page in ten years, every line should still be true and kind. Rewrite anything that fails that test.
